Privacy policy

Controller: Jannis Schüler, Dorfstraße 8, 06862 Dessau-Roßlau OT Mühlstedt, support@daily-questcom.com

Progress on your device

Tasks, progress, character and settings are stored on your device. Without an account, we don't transfer this data to us. If you delete the app, it's gone.

User account (optional)

If you sign in, we store your email address and a copy of your progress so you don't lose it and can keep playing on other devices. For this we use Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA) with servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. We have a data processing agreement with Supabase. The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We only use your email address for sign-in codes. These emails are sent via Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA) with servers in Ireland; we also have a data processing agreement with Resend. You can delete your account in Settings at any time; your email address and cloud progress are then deleted immediately.

Reminders

The app schedules reminders directly on your device. It asks for your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We don't receive any payment details. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to process purchases. This involves a random identifier without your name, the products purchased and technical device data. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

App updates

The app downloads bug fixes and small improvements through Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA). To do so, it checks for a new version when it starts. This processes your IP address, the app version and technical device data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a reliable and secure app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Friends (optional)

With an account you can add friends by code. For your friends we then store the name you choose, your level, your streak and how many quests you completed today and this week, as well as who cheered on whom. This is only visible to people you added as a friend or who added you. We do not share your quests themselves. Storage is with Supabase as described above. The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can remove friends at any time; deleting your account deletes everything.

Apple Health (optional)

If you connect the app to Apple Health, it reads today's steps, distance, water, workouts and mindful minutes on your iPhone to check off matching quests. This health data never leaves your device and is not transferred to us or any third party. You can withdraw permission in the Health app at any time.

Error reports and statistics

To find bugs and improve the app, it sends anonymous error reports and usage events (e.g. "intro completed" or "level reached") together with the app version, operating system and language to our database at Supabase. There is no persistent identifier, no email address and no link to your account; each session gets a new random ID. Your IP address is processed for the transfer for technical reasons but not stored with the entries. Entries are deleted after 180 days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a bug-free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can turn this off in Settings under Privacy.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads, uses no third-party analytics services and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To do so, write to us at the address above.
